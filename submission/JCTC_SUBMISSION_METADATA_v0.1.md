**Working title:** Chemical Fidelity beyond Pointwise Error Bounds: Stability and Bader-Domain Migration in Lossy-Compressed Electron Densities

## Short editor pitch

A stability-qualified computational methodology is developed for deciding when lossy-compressed DFT electron densities remain valid inputs to Bader charge analysis. The study separates nominal from realized field error, re-executes field-dependent partitioning, and requires the Bader observable itself to be numerically resolvable before certification.
