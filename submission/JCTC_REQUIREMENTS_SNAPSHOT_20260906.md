# JCTC / ACS Submission Requirements Snapshot

**Checked:** 2026-09-06  
**Purpose:** internal submission-preparation record. Re-check live journal guidance on the actual submission day.

## Journal and submission route

- Target journal: *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation* (JCTC).
- Manuscript type: **Article**.
- Submission route: **ACS Publishing Center**.
- Journal scope explicitly includes new theories, methods, and important applications in quantum electronic structure and computational chemistry.

## Initial-submission formatting

ACS Fast Format permits a review-ready initial submission without full production styling, provided that:

- standard sections for original research are clearly identified;
- figures, charts, tables, schemes, and equations are embedded in the manuscript near their first discussion when possible;
- references are complete and include titles; initial submission does not require final journal-specific reference typography;
- Supporting Information is uploaded as a separate file;
- manuscript author names and affiliations match the metadata entered in the ACS Publishing Center.

## Table-of-Contents graphic

JCTC requires a graphical summary for the Table of Contents.

Working production specification for this submission package:

- canvas: **3.25 in wide × 1.75 in high**;
- original artwork only;
- short labels rather than paragraph text;
- must remain legible after reduction;
- label/file designation should follow the current ACS portal instructions, including “For Table of Contents Only” where requested.

The exact file-format/resolution requirement should be checked in the live ACS submission portal immediately before upload.

## Supporting Information

Supporting Information should be supplied separately from the main manuscript. It should contain reviewer-facing supplementary methods, sensitivity analyses, tables, figures, and reproducibility detail that are not needed in the main narrative.

## Data and software reproducibility

A 2026 joint JCTC/JCIM editorial states that, effective May 1, 2026, original research manuscripts should include a **Data and Software Availability Statement**. Data, scripts, code, and supporting materials needed to reproduce key results should be made available at manuscript submission whenever possible, preferably in machine-readable form and through repositories with persistent identifiers.

For this paper, the preferred implementation is:

`submission commit -> immutable Git tag/release -> Zenodo or equivalent archive -> persistent DOI`

The archive should include the successful benchmark, failure registry, stability/probe data, mechanism data actually used in the paper, provenance metadata, supplementary tables, statistical scripts, and figure-generation inputs.

## Graphics and originality

- Main figures should be embedded in the initial manuscript where possible.
- Final high-quality figure files should be retained separately for revision/production.
- Previously published artwork requires appropriate credit/permission; the TOC graphic must be an original creation.
- The journal production team does not repair weak scientific graphics, so final axes, typography, line weights, and raster/vector quality must be publication-ready.

## Administrative items to confirm before submission

- final author order;
- author names and affiliations;
- corresponding-author contact details;
- ORCID identifiers;
- funding sources and grant numbers;
- conflict-of-interest declaration;
- confirmation that all authors approve the submission;
- confirmation that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere;
- disclosure of related manuscripts or preprints where applicable;
- exact repository/archive DOI and submitted commit SHA.
