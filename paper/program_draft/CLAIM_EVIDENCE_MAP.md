## How to read this map

- Every number is copied from the file named in the "source" column. "this branch" means
  `research/general-qoac-law-confirm-20261006` (and therefore this branch). "paper branch" means
  `origin/paper/qoac-integration-20261005`. Commit hashes are the last commit that changed the file.
- Evidence status:
  - **prospective-confirmed**: hypotheses, criteria and (where applicable) predictions were frozen and
    committed before data were generated on a population never used for development; all criteria passed.
  - **confirmatory**: a frozen protocol on a disjoint holdout or untouched cohort; passed. The population
    may come from the same source pool as the development data.
  - **engineering-only**: development data, gates frozen before execution but not confirmed on a holdout.
  - **retrospective**: explanation, census or calibration computed after the outcome was known.
  - **failed / NO-GO**: a pre-registered gate or criterion was not met.
- The prior-art column uses the audits:
  - `paper/QOAC_PRIOR_ART_NOVELTY_AUDIT_20261005.md` (this branch, `e57a8ce`; called "audit-H");
  - `paper/QOAC_PROGRAM_PRIOR_ART_AUDIT_20261006.md` and its `_VERIFICATION.md` on
    `origin/research/prior-art-program-20261006` (`4bf9bad`, `ebfe465`; called "audit-P", claims C1–C4).
- **Forbidden everywhere** (audit-H "Avoid"; audit-P "Wording to avoid everywhere"): "first QoI-aware /
  operator-aware / physics-informed / frequency-weighted compressor", "first compression method for electron
  density or Hartree potential", "novel" attached to RDO, coding gain, projection or multi-QoI control,
  "new bit-allocation method", "first multi-QoI certified compression". Neither audit licenses "first" or
  "novel" for any claim below. No row of this map uses them as permitted wording.
- The **risk** column is for the authors and reviewers' anticipated objections. It is not manuscript text.
