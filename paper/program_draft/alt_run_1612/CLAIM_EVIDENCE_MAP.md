## How to read this map

- **Branch / commit.** "law-confirm" = `origin/research/general-qoac-law-confirm-20261006` (this branch's base). "frozen-paper" =
  `origin/paper/qoac-integration-20261005` @ `10d3d37`. "prior-art" = `origin/research/prior-art-program-20261006` @ `ebfe465`.
  "b3-design" = `origin/research/qoac-b3-design-20261006` @ `94d06cf`. The hash given per row is the last commit that
  touched the cited result file.
- **Evidence status** uses five labels:
  - **prospective-confirmed:** the protocol, and where relevant the predictions, were committed before any outcome on a
    never-used population, and every pre-registered criterion passed;
  - **confirmatory:** a frozen protocol on a held-out cohort passed its pre-registered criteria, but the cohort or design
    is weaker than prospective (non-naive cohort, or no separate prediction step);
  - **engineering-only:** development data, gates frozen before the run, no confirmation authorized or run;
  - **retrospective:** analysis or prediction written after the outcomes were known (mechanism audits, calibrations,
    censuses of the development population);
  - **failed or NO-GO:** a frozen gate or criterion failed.
- **Prior-art sources.** "PA-H" = `paper/QOAC_PRIOR_ART_NOVELTY_AUDIT_20261005.md` (prior-art branch; also on frozen-paper).
  "PA-P" = `paper/QOAC_PROGRAM_PRIOR_ART_AUDIT_20261006.md`, sections C1–C4. "PA-V" = its `_VERIFICATION.md`.
- **Global wording rule from PA-H and PA-P.** "first" and "novel" are licensed for **no** claim in this map. The audits
  allow only a scoped positioning sentence ("no retrieved work …"), and never as an absolute priority claim.
- **Timing note.** PA-P (`ebfe465`, 2026-10-06 15:35 UTC) was committed about 21 minutes before the P1 confirmation was
  recorded (`f10490f`, 15:56 UTC). Its C1/C2 verdicts ("development only", "nothing predicted prospectively", "finite-rate
  model not built or tested") are therefore superseded **for the P1 cohort only**. The audit must be updated before any of
  rows A1–A8 below is written into a manuscript. The credit lists of PA-P stay as they are.
