## Final claim hierarchy for the manuscript

The preferred evidence chain is:

1. **Measurement correction:** re-run the downstream Bader analysis; fixed basins understate error.
2. **Numerical qualification:** establish whether the requested Bader contract is defined under Protocol A.1.
3. **Distortion control:** distinguish nominal tolerance from measured realized L∞.
4. **Mechanism:** matched-L∞ residual + basin reassignment attenuation + direct domain decomposition.
5. **Independent dataset:** all three directional rate–fidelity predictions reproduce on the frozen 63-system cohort.
6. **Independent implementation:** Henkelman reproduces the central perturbation sensitivity and codec ordering.
7. **Spatial control:** rearranging an unchanged error-value multiset changes the response, while periodic translation is approximately null.

The manuscript should therefore lead with the methodological conclusion rather than a codec leaderboard:

> **Pointwise error control is a reconstruction guarantee, not by itself a chemical-fidelity guarantee. Trustworthy scientific compression requires a stability-qualified downstream contract, measured realized distortion, re-execution of the downstream analysis, and validation that the resulting decision is robust to independent data and implementation.**
