# Figure 8 caption — QOAC-H diagnosis-to-design

**Figure 8 | The downstream Hartree operator converts compression diagnosis into design.**
**a**, The periodic Hartree response weights squared reciprocal-space density error as $|G|^{-4}$; under the frozen high-rate scalar-quantization model this gives the first-order allocation $\Delta_G\propto|G|^2$, protecting the long-wavelength modes that dominate Hartree error while allowing progressively coarser high-$G$ quantization. **b**, Pre-specified matched-storage ablation on 12 engineering materials. The operator-derived exponent $\beta=2$ gives lower Hartree error than operator-blind $\beta=0$ in 12/12 materials; the material-median error ratio is 0.0767. **c**, Disjoint confirmation on 48 previously unseen materials at relative Hartree RMSE $<10^{-6}$. QOAC-H beats each material's best certified ZFP/SZ3/SPERR baseline in 48/48 cases; the median compression-ratio advantage is 15.016-fold (bootstrap 95% CI, 11.204–21.461), with bulk and slab medians of 11.203-fold and 25.699-fold. All 48 selected QOAC-H reconstructions also satisfy the Nyquist-safe Hartree guardrail. **d**, Complete 254-material development-population census across Hartree tolerances from $10^{-8}$ to $10^{-3}$. Points are median QOAC-H/best-baseline compression-ratio ratios, thick intervals are the interquartile range and thin intervals are P05–P95; labels give the number of comparable material–tolerance pairs. At $10^{-6}$, QOAC-H wins in 253/253 comparable materials, with median 12.463-fold, P05 4.459-fold and minimum 2.444-fold. QSQ eligibility and availability of a certified frozen baseline define the denominator at each tolerance.
