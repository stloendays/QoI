# Frozen conclusion — matched realized-L∞ V1

The equal-nominal comparison is materially confounded by codec-dependent use of
the requested pointwise error budget. ZFP realizes only about one-sixth of the
L∞ perturbation of SZ3/SPERR at the same nominal tolerance.

After controlling realized L∞ within material, the large gap contracts but does
not vanish: at the primary 0.10-dex caliper, resolved Bader error is 0.557× for
ZFP/SZ3 and 0.601× for ZFP/SPERR, while SZ3/SPERR is 1.033×. The residual is
stable over 0.05–0.30 dex.

Therefore the supported claim is:

**A scalar L∞ bound does not determine chemical fidelity. Equal nominal tolerance
is an important confound, and error-field structure contributes beyond the
maximum pointwise amplitude.**
